Design decisions and why
  1. Rules first, AI optional. Every answer points to a Sheet row, so it can be tested and explained. 48 tests
  2. The owner's Sheet is the database. No new app; a price edit applies on the next reply.
  3. Verify at the edge. Nothing is read or stored before Meta's signature matches. 167739e
  4. Quiet by default. The owner is emailed for orders and unknown questions, plus one summary at 6 pm. run #48, #49
When something goes wrong failure paths
  • builtUnsigned or tampered request: dropped with 403, nothing stored.
  • builtQuestion no rule knows: routed as "other", owner emailed to answer it.
  • builtWorkflow export going public: the cleaner refuses any email address. 383a06d
  • plannedPrice left blank in the Sheet: "message us" instead of a guess.
