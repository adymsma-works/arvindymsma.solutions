axe after all fixes (reduced motion so fade-ins are checked, 11 pages x 390 and 1366 px): **no violations**.

**Item 4 (home mobile) needed no fix.** Measured style and layout on a 4x slowed phone: ~90 ms style + ~310 ms
layout; switching off blur, video, animations, mocks, container queries or glow one at a time changed nothing beyond
noise. Lighthouse 5 runs on `main` vs the branch: same (median perf 100, TBT ~60 ms). Live site 3 more runs: perf
85-98, TBT 117-587 ms. The first run's 77 was one bad sample. Lesson: one Lighthouse run is not a number; take
the median of 3-5, and confirm with a real throttled browser.
